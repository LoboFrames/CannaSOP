9-week arc

Key operating rules

UV
  • Split into two daily sessions (~1.5h morning + ~1.5h afternoon) rather than one continuous block.
  • Mid-cycle only — never the first hour after lights-on or the last hour before lights-off.
  • Keep ambient temp under 84°F (29°C) whenever UV is active.
  • Pause 3–5 days after any defoliation pass, resume once recovered.
  • Stop entirely 5 days before harvest.
Far-Red
  • Running with the main spectrum triggers the Emerson effect — meaningfully boosts photosynthetic efficiency.
  • Cut starting week 7 — buds are bulking and no longer need the stretch signal.
  • Worth testing on your own genetics: some cultivars hold density fine with a low dose kept through weeks 7–8.
CO2 & EC
  • CO2 only useful while lights are on — shut off in sync with lights-off.
  • High PPFD without matching CO2 just wastes electricity — raise both together.
  • Check runoff EC weekly; flush immediately above 3.5 mS/cm.
Harvest
  • Target 70–80% cloudy trichomes with 10–20% amber for peak quality.
