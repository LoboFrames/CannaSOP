How the schedule works排程运作原理

Day 8 is the target, not the deadline. Consistency is worth real money downstream — the trim room, Rob's machine schedule, and the aunties all plan around a Monday handoff. 第8天是目标，不是死线。一致性在下游意味着实实在在的效益——修剪房、Rob 的机器排期以及阿姨们都围绕周一交接安排工作。

Day 12 is the outer bound. Some batches will not be ready on Day 8, and forcing them down wet is worse than extending. Drying rate is the water load in the room against the capacity to remove it, and both vary: plant count, wet weight, leaf load, whether the room was wet-trimmed, how many dehumidifiers are running, whether AC control is holding. Day 12 is not a forced takedown — it is where the call stops being the technician's and becomes a documented decision by the Lead and the Supervisor. 第12天是最终期限。有些批次到第8天不会达标，湿着强行下架比延长更糟。干燥速度取决于房间内的水分负荷与除湿能力之比，两者都会变化：株数、湿重、叶量、是否湿修、除湿机运行台数、空调控制是否稳定。第12天并非强制下架——而是该判断不再由技术员决定，转为由主管与上级主管共同作出并记录在案的决策。

Most of that is knowable on Day 1其中大部分在第1天就可预知 Plant count, poundage, and leaf load are recorded at harvest. Dehumidifier count and AC status are known before the room is loaded. A heavy load should be predicted at intake, not discovered on Monday morning. 株数、重量和叶量在采收时已记录。除湿机数量与空调状态在装载前已知。重载批次应在入库时预判，而不是在周一早晨才发现。
The target is a window, not a point目标是区间，而非单点 The Pre-Trim Cure downstream (1–3 days, moisture-driven) absorbs the remainder. The dry room needs to land inside the cure's entry window and hand off an accurate reading — not hit the final number. 下游预修剪熟化（1–3天，由含水量决定）会吸收剩余差异。干燥房只需落在熟化的入口区间内并准确传递读数，无需达到最终数值。

The outer bound landing on Friday is convenient: a Day 12 takedown means bucking Friday, cure across the weekend, machine trim Monday. The cure absorbs the weekend either way. 最终期限落在周五很合适：第12天下架意味着周五摘花、周末熟化、周一机器修剪。无论哪条路径，熟化都能吸收周末时间。

Extension holds Phase 2 conditions — it does not continue Phase 3延长期维持第2阶段条件——不是延续第3阶段 Phase 3 is a takedown-day hold; its job is to stop the flower drying while the crew works. Applying it to a batch that still needs to lose water stalls the dry and burns a day. If the Day 8 gate fails, the room goes back to 65°F / 45% the same morning. 第3阶段是下架当天的保持条件，作用是在作业期间阻止花继续失水。将其用于仍需失水的批次会停滞干燥、浪费一天。若第8天判定未通过，当天早晨即将房间调回 65°F / 45%。
No fan points at hanging flower风扇不得直吹挂晾的花 Fans move air through the room; they do not blow on plants. Some facing up, some pushing across. Direct airflow dries the exterior faster than moisture can migrate out of the interior. 风扇的作用是让空气在房间内流动，而不是吹向植株。部分朝上，部分横向推动空气。直吹会使表面干燥速度快于内部水分迁移速度。

Day 5 checkpoint — Friday · mandatory第5天检查点——周五 · 必检

The last day a change can meaningfully move the Day 8 result. It does not get skipped, and the Lead is notified either way — including when no action was needed. 最后一个能实质影响第8天结果的调整日。不得跳过，且无论结果如何都须通知主管——包括无需行动的情况。

  • Reweigh all three tagged branches. Calculate percent loss from their harvest day wet weight.重新称量全部三根标记枝。计算相对于采收日湿重的失重百分比。
  • Take pin meter readings. Probe through the bud shoulder toward the stem, pins fully sunk. Average 3–5 buds per point, across at least four positions. Second reading on a thin twig at each point. Wipe pins with 70% IPA between samples.进行针式水分仪读数。从花肩部朝茎方向插入，探针完全没入。每点取3–5个花头平均值，至少四个位置。每点另取一次细枝读数。每次取样间用70%异丙醇擦拭探针。

Readiness gate — Day 8 onward, run daily准备判定——第8天起，每日执行

All three checks are taken and recorded. From Day 8 on, this gate runs daily until the batch passes, or reaches Day 12 and escalates. 三项检查全部执行并记录。自第8天起每日执行，直至批次通过，或到达第12天转为上报决策。

One meter, and it is the reference只有一台水分仪，且它就是基准 The room runs a single dedicated FLIR MR40. No pin meter sold is calibrated for cannabis — they all read a wood scale — so the 11–12% band is this meter's number on this flower. Substituting another meter mid-cycle, even the same model, moves the band without telling you. Check the pins are straight and clean before the cycle starts. 本房间仅使用一台专属的 FLIR MR40。市售针式水分仪均未按大麻校准——读的都是木材刻度——因此 11–12% 区间是这台仪器在这批花上的读数。周期中途更换仪器，即使是同型号，也会在无声中改变该区间。周期开始前请检查探针是否平直、洁净。

Day 12 is a mandatory escalation, not an automatic takedown第12天为强制上报，而非自动下架 Standing policy is that flower does not come down until it is ready, so Day 12 does not force the batch down — it forces a decision. Run the full gate, escalate to the Lead and the Supervisor the same day, and record the decision with its probable cause: load size, dehumidification, AC, hang density. What gets weighed against readiness is mold risk, the room being needed for Monday's harvest, and a downstream schedule that has already slipped. Tell the trim room lead and Rob the same day, not on the new takedown morning. 既定政策是花未达标不下架，因此第12天并不强制批次下架，而是强制作出决策。执行完整判定，当天上报主管与上级主管，并记录决定及可能原因：负荷量、除湿、空调、挂晾密度。需与达标程度一并权衡的是霉菌风险、周一采收需要腾出房间，以及已经延后的下游排程。当天即告知修剪房负责人与 Rob，而非等到新的下架日早晨。

Handoff to post-processing交接至后处理

Takedown feeds directly into Stage 1 of the Post-Processing SOP. 下架直接进入后处理 SOP 的第1阶段。

Flower goes straight to the trim room花直接进入修剪房 It does not sit in the buck room, a hallway, or on the dry room floor after take-down. Bucking happens in the trim room at 58–62°F / 55–60% RH — the buck room cannot hold that target, the trim room can. 下架后不得停留在摘花房、走廊或干燥房地面。摘花在修剪房进行，条件为 58–62°F / 55–60% RH——摘花房无法维持该目标，修剪房可以。
  • 27 gal tote, 6–7 lb max during cure. Do not begin bucking until the tote is full.27加仑周转箱，熟化期间最多 6–7 磅。周转箱装满前不要开始摘花。
  • Pass the takedown-day readings forward on the batch record. Rob judges cure length by moisture — the entry moisture is the starting point for that judgment, and he should not have to guess at it.将下架当天的读数写入批次记录并传递下去。Rob 依据含水量判断熟化时长——入口含水率是该判断的起点，不应让他靠猜测。
  • On any extended cycle, tell the trim room lead verbally as well as on paper — and tell them as early as the Day 5 or Day 7 read, not on the takedown day.任何延长周期都须口头告知修剪房负责人，而不仅是书面记录——且应在第5天或第7天读数时就告知，而非等到下架当天。

Troubleshooting故障排除

Symptom现象 Action措施
Room will not hold RH房间无法维持湿度 Check dehumidifier drains, filters, and door seal. If the fresh air intake is bringing in wet air, reduce the exchange rate and notify the Lead. Do not chase it with temperature.检查除湿机排水、滤网及门封。若新风进气带入湿空气，降低换气量并通知主管。不要用温度去补偿。
Actual RH consistently above setpoint实际湿度持续高于设定值 Capacity, not setpoint. Lowering the dial will not help. Add portables, cut exchange rate, check for an iced or failed unit, verify AC. This is the single most common cause of a batch needing extension.这是能力问题，不是设定值问题。调低数值无济于事。增加移动除湿机、降低换气量、检查是否结冰或故障、确认空调。这是批次需要延长的最常见原因。
TrolMaster disagrees with the orange handheldTrolMaster 与橙色手持仪读数不符 Recalibrate the sensor. Do not compensate by changing setpoints — you will be flying blind for the rest of the cycle.重新校准传感器。不要通过调整设定值来补偿——那样整个周期都将失去参照。
Mold or botrytis found发现霉菌或灰霉病 Remove and isolate immediately. Do not carry it through the room. Notify the Lead. Increase airflow; do not raise temperature.立即移除并隔离。不要携带其穿过房间。通知主管。加大气流；不要升高温度。
Crispy exterior, soft interior外表干脆、内部柔软 Case hardening — the dry is outrunning moisture migration. Raise RH immediately and reduce fan speed. Note it on the batch record and plan for a longer cure.表面硬化——干燥速度超过了水分迁移速度。立即提高湿度并降低风扇转速。记入批次记录并安排更长的熟化时间。
Meter readings scattered widely across the room房间内水分仪读数差异很大 An airflow or hang-density problem, not a meter problem. Reposition fans, open up crowded sections, note the affected positions.这是气流或挂晾密度问题，不是仪器问题。调整风扇位置，疏散拥挤区域，记录受影响位置。
Tagged branches disagree by more than about 10%标记枝之间差异超过约 10% The room is drying unevenly. Record which positions are fast and slow, and correct fan placement before the next cycle.房间干燥不均。记录哪些位置偏快、哪些偏慢，并在下一周期前调整风扇布置。
Batch extends past Day 10 with no clear cause批次延长至第10天后仍无明确原因 Pull the whole cycle's setpoint-versus-actual record before changing anything. The answer is almost always in that column.在做任何调整之前，先调出整个周期的设定值与实际值记录。答案几乎总在那一栏里。
